# Wobble Isle: web pages

The privacy policy and support pages of Wobble Isle, a party game for iPhone by E2 PARTNERS L.L.C-FZ. Their public home is the company site, which copies them from here:

- Privacy policy: https://e2partners.co/wobble-isle/privacy/ (here: https://ege90.github.io/wobble-isle-site/privacy/)
- Support: https://e2partners.co/wobble-isle/support/ (here: https://ege90.github.io/wobble-isle-site/support/)

They are kept with the game and published here from there (`tools/site/publish.sh`), so change them there, not here. The fonts are Lilita One and Nunito, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, whose texts are beside them in `fonts/`.
