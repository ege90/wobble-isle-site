Wobble Isle

A party game for four on iPhone: short arena mini-games at a music festival on a floating island, with friends in a room or against bots.

Throw boxes in Box Brawl, ride the ice in Shove Off, paint the floor in Splat Hop and race for the Golden Record in Relic Run. One joystick and two buttons in every game.
