Wobble Isle

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Wobble Isle asks for no name, email, password or contacts, shows no ads and does not track you. To play online it uses an anonymous player ID, which you can delete from inside the game at any time.

Wobble Isle is a party game for iPhone made by E2 PARTNERS L.L.C-FZ (“we”). This page says what the game collects, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept, and how to delete it.

What the game keeps on your phone

Your choices: your character, the mode, how well the bots play, and the mini-games you picked. Also a random number that lets a room give you back your seat if your connection drops, and the sign-in for online play. They stay on your phone, except that the random number goes to the host of a room you join.

What online play uses

Playing against bots needs no connection and sends nothing. When you host, join or look for a room:

  • An anonymous player ID. Unity Authentication makes it the first time your phone goes online. It is not linked to your name, your email or your Apple ID.
  • Your IP address. Unity’s servers see it when your phone connects, as any internet service does, and use it to carry the game between the phones in a room.
  • The game itself. Your moves, your character and the room’s settings travel between the phones in the room while you play. They are not stored.
  • Open rooms. A room its host lets anyone join is listed with its code, its games and settings, the host’s character and the host’s anonymous player ID, so that other players can find it. The listing is removed when the room closes.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, precise location, advertising identifiers or payment details. The game has no chat, no ads and no analytics, and nothing in it tracks you across other apps or websites.

Why

Only to run online play: to sign your phone in, connect you to rooms, give you back your seat after a dropped connection and show open rooms. Never for advertising or profiles, and never sold.

Who else handles it

Unity Technologies runs the online services for us: Unity Authentication, Unity Relay and Unity Lobby. Unity handles the data on our behalf under its data processing terms, which require it to protect the data at least as well as this policy does. How Unity handles data is in Unity’s privacy policy. Unity’s servers are in several countries, including the United States.

Other players in a room see your character and how you play. Nobody else receives your data from us.

How long it is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it, or delete the game.
  • Your player ID at Unity: until you delete it from the game, or ask us to.
  • Unity Relay and Unity Lobby: IP addresses and player IDs in their records for up to 30 days.
  • Open-room listings: until the room closes.

Deleting your data

In the game, tap the round button at the top left of the first screen, then Delete my data. It deletes your player ID at Unity and everything the game keeps on your phone, and the game starts afresh as on a new phone.

You can also write to us with your player ID, shown at the bottom left of that page, and we will delete it. Deleting the game from your phone removes what it kept there, but not your player ID at Unity: use Delete my data first. You can stop sharing anything with us at any time by playing only against bots.

Children

The game has no chat, no names, no ads and no purchases. It uses the anonymous player ID and IP address only to run online play, never to contact anyone, to advertise or to build profiles. If you think a child has sent us anything more, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct or delete your data, or to object to how it is used. We hold no names or contact details, so we find your data by your player ID: write to us with it. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

When this policy changes, this page and its date change with it.
