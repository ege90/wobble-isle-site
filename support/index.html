Common questions

How do I play with friends?

Tap Host a room and share its code, or tap Join and type a friend’s code. Find a room lists rooms that anyone can join. Empty seats get bots, and a bot is always labelled as one.

It says “The host has a different version of the game”

Everyone in a room needs the same version. Update Wobble Isle from the App Store on every phone, then try again.

It can’t get online

Hosting, joining and finding rooms need an internet connection. Matches against bots work without one.

How do I play?

Move with the joystick under your left thumb, and use the two buttons under your right: one for the mini-game’s action and one to dash. A game controller works too.

How do I delete my data?

Tap the round button at the top left of the first screen, then Delete my data. The privacy policy says what it deletes.
